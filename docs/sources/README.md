## 5. Farrin 2014 — Structure and Qur'anic Interpretation (book)

- Not archived here (all rights reserved); PDF:
  https://archive.org/download/structure-and-quranic-interpretation-a-study-of-symmetry-and-coherence-in-islams-holy-text-1/Structure_and_Quranic_Interpretation-A_Study_of_Symmetry_and_Coherence_in_Islams_Holy_Text%20(1).pdf
- Raymond Farrin (American University of Kuwait / AUS)
- "Structure and Qur'anic Interpretation: A Study of Symmetry and Coherence
  in Islam's Holy Text", White Cloud Press, Ashland OR, 2014, 163 pp. print
- ISBN 978-1-935952-98-5 (print), 978-1-935952-99-2 (ebook)
- URL (open copy): https://archive.org/download/structure-and-quranic-interpretation-a-study-of-symmetry-and-coherence-in-islams-holy-text-1/
- Also: Kabakci 2023 review of Farrin (DergiPark, open access, not archived
  here: https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/download/article-file/548285)
- Build on: Cuypers/Meynet (ring composition: parallelism AB/A'B', chiasm
  AB/B'A', concentrism AB/C/B'A'), Islahi (chapter pairs), Mary Douglas
  ("the meaning is in the middle"), al-Farahi/Tabataba'i/Qutb lineage.
- STRUCTURE (testable against mushaf data): 19 chapter groups in two
  concentric systems + one central group.
  System A: a(2-5) b(6-9) c(10-15) d(16-21) e(22-24) | d'(25-32) c'(33-39)
            b'(40-46) a'(47-49)
  Central (Hereafter): 50-56
  System B: a(57-66) b(67-72) c(73-80) d(81-88) e(89-92) | d'(93-98)
            c'(99-104) b'(105-108) a'(109-112)
  Correspondences: same group; ring-mirror (x <-> x' within a system);
  system-mirror (A-letter <-> B-letter).
- Relevance: literary-structure school = the meaning-side twin of the
  tawafuq mushaf's visual design. Our pair census (tools/pair_census.py)
  finds marked-word cross-page positional pairs enriched 1.3-1.9x over
  real-geometry null on Farrin-correspondent surah pairs.
